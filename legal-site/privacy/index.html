Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Auvellis helps you plan a goal toward a date you choose. It stores the account and progress needed to provide that service. Auvellis has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell data.

Who is responsible

Shiri Laz is responsible for Auvellis. Contact and support: appdevqyv@gmail.com.

Information we store

  • Account: email, authentication data managed by Supabase Auth, and optional display name.
  • Goal and plan: goal text, target date, identity answer, planning pace, daily time budget, blockers, personalisation answers, stages, practice titles, steps and plan versions.
  • Progress: practice completion status, local date, minutes, optional effort rating and note; weekly review difficulty and optional note.
  • Safety choices: self-declared age bracket (13–17 or 18+), conservative-mode flag and goal category. The physical-goal screening answer is used in the moment and is not stored. These safeguards are not a medical assessment, and age is not verified.
  • On your device: reminder time, scheduled local notifications and offline practice records waiting to sync.

We do not collect contacts, location, advertising identifiers, analytics events, health records or payment data through the app.

Why we use information

We use account information to sign you in; goal and preference information to create and adapt your plan; progress information to show streaks, milestones and weekly suggestions; and reminder settings to schedule notifications on your device.

AI suggestions

Only after you agree, Auvellis may send your goal, identity answer, target date, planning checkpoint, daily time budget, blockers and optional personalisation answers to our server and Google Gemini to draft questions or suggestions. Weekly requests can include current practice, progress, effort and review notes. Emails, phone numbers and @handles are removed before sending, but this is redaction, not full anonymisation. Remaining details can still identify you. Avoid sensitive health or confidential details.

Auvellis uses the paid Gemini API service for AI suggestions. Google’s paid-service terms say prompts and responses are not used to improve its products. Google may still process prompts and responses for abuse monitoring for a limited period. See Google Gemini API terms.

If you decline or withdraw AI permission, future AI requests stop. Existing plan text may remain. A complete on-device plan remains available without AI.

Storage and retention

Account and progress data are stored in Supabase Postgres with row-level security. We retain account data while your account exists. Archived seasons remain in your history until you delete your account. After deletion, data is removed from the live database; encrypted infrastructure backups may retain copies until they expire under Supabase’s backup schedule for the service plan.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device after you choose a time and allow notifications. You can change or turn them off in Auvellis Settings or in system settings. They are cancelled on sign-out, AI consent withdrawal and account deletion.

Children and teens

Auvellis is not for children under 13, who cannot create an account. Age is self-reported and is not independently verified by the app. People aged 13–17 may use Auvellis only where permitted by the laws that apply to them, and with parent or guardian permission where required. Auvellis does not verify age or collect parental permission in the app. Teen users are encouraged to involve a trusted adult, especially for physical goals.

Account deletion

Settings → Delete my account permanently deletes the authentication account and associated profile, seasons, practices, milestones, events, reviews, plan versions, consent fields and quota records from the live database. Device-only offline records and reminders are cleared on that device. Deletion cannot be undone. Encrypted infrastructure backups may retain copies until they expire under the provider’s backup schedule.

Changes and contact

We will show material policy changes in the app before they take effect. For privacy questions, contact appdevqyv@gmail.com or visit Support.
