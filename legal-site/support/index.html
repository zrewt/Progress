Email appdevqyv@gmail.com. Include the email address on your Auvellis account and a short description of the issue. Never send your password, sign-in codes, payment information, or sensitive health details.

Common questions

I can’t sign in

Check that you’re using the email address you registered with. Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen and check your spam folder if the reset email doesn’t arrive.

My progress didn’t sync

Reopen Auvellis when you have an internet connection. Practices recorded offline remain queued on your device until they sync. Avoid deleting the app before they sync, since the offline queue is stored on that device.

How do I manage reminders?

Open Settings → Reminders, or turn notifications off in your device’s system settings.

How do I manage AI personalisation?

Open Settings → AI personalisation. Withdrawing permission stops future AI requests. Existing plan text remains unless you edit or delete your account.

How do I delete my account?

Use the account and privacy controls in Settings. Account deletion is permanent. Contact appdevqyv@gmail.com if you need help.
